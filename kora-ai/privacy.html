Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy describes the personal information handled by Kora AI, a wellness application provided by Jana Saadeh.

Account and profile

Firebase Authentication handles email and password sign-in or Google Sign-In. Your user identifier, email, display name and profile photo or photo URL are stored in Cloud Firestore to provide your account and profile.

Journaling and voice

With microphone permission, Kora AI records audio during check-ins in temporary WAV files on your device. Speech emotion inference uses an on-device TensorFlow Lite model; the journal save implementation sends analysis results and an amplitude curve rather than the raw audio file to Firestore. Saved journal records can include timestamps, selected reasons, text sentiment scores, emotion analysis, vitality scores, heart rate, step totals and city/country. These records support journal history, wellness insights and recommendations. Draft notes, context text, reasons and health/activity values can be stored locally using SharedPreferences.

Health Connect

With your permission, Kora AI reads steps, heart rate, active calories and workout sessions from Android Health Connect to calculate and display activity and wellness insights. Calories and activity minutes may also be estimated from steps. Heart rate, step totals and derived wellness scores may be stored in your Firebase journal records. You can revoke access in Health Connect settings at any time. Revocation does not erase previously saved entries. Kora AI does not write health records to Health Connect. Health information is used to provide wellness features, not for advertising or sale.

Location

With location permission, the app obtains device coordinates and uses platform geocoding to resolve a city and country for journal context. The saved journal model stores the city/country label rather than raw coordinates. You may deny or revoke location access.

Service providers and protection

Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud database services; Google Sign-In and Android platform services support the features described above. Providers process information to deliver these services and may process technical information needed for their operation. See Firebase privacy information. Account identifiers are used to associate cloud records with your account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Retention, deletion and choices

Cloud account and journal records are kept to provide your account and history until you request deletion or they are otherwise removed. To request deletion of your account and associated data, follow the account deletion instructions. Information required for legal or security obligations may be retained only as necessary. Local drafts, preferences and temporary recordings can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Android Health Connect data held separately is managed through Health Connect. You can also manage microphone, location and notification permissions in Android settings.

Contact

For privacy questions and data requests, email jana.saadeh.dev@gmail.com.

Kora AI provides general wellness support and is not a medical diagnosis or treatment service.
