KORA AI

Delete your account

To request permanent deletion of your Kora AI account and associated personal data, contact the developer, Jana Saadeh. You do not need to sign in to the app to submit a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email from the address associated with your Kora AI account, if possible.
  2. Use the subject “Kora AI account deletion request” and identify your account. Never send your password.
  3. We will verify account ownership and confirm the status of your request by email.

Request account deletion

Contact: jana.saadeh.dev@gmail.com

What deletion covers

Deletion requests cover your Firebase Authentication account, Firestore profile (including stored profile photo), journal entries, saved heart rate and steps, city/country, emotion analysis and vitality scores associated with your account. Any information required for legal or security obligations may be retained only as necessary.

Processing

Requests are handled manually after verification. We will provide an expected completion date and notify you when deletion is complete. Local app drafts and temporary recordings can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling Kora AI. Health records independently held by Health Connect are not removed by deleting your Kora AI account; manage those in Health Connect settings.
